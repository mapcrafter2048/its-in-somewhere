# Personal desk expansion

Press **N** for your notebook. Notes, Discoveries, Collection and Shift log are its four sections. The original archive task is independent of every activity below.

## Personal stationery

The notebook keeps one cloth cover and four small horizontal page flags. Contents opens an entry list; page controls turn equal facing leaves on wide windows, or a single leaf below 900px. Collection and Shift log are pages of this same book. All 12 sheets are available to turn through immediately, including blank sheets. Write on the left and sketch or review clippings on the right; Read pages opens a paginated reading view for long notes. Blank browsing does not consume capacity or increase written-sheet counters. On narrow windows, turn between writing and sketch leaves. The notebook remembers its section and reading position for the current session.

The notebook starts with 12 reusable sheets. Sheets support typed notes, four paper colours, four pen colours, sketch erasing and stroke undo. **Personal stationery** costs 24 supply credits and expands capacity to 36. Sheet positions remain stable. Recycling leaves that numbered slot blank; deleting written work requires confirmation.

**Clip to notebook** in a reader retains its visible page or workbook cells with a filing reference. Hidden rows stay out until restored, and only an inspected cell comment can be included. The original record is never changed.

Return to the physical pinboard beside the W4 utility table, or open it from Your desk. Tear out sheets, drag them, move a focused sheet with arrow keys, connect two sheets using the labelled connection form, and return sheets to the notebook. Pinned sheets can be edited at the board; elsewhere their notebook entry is read-only. Arrangement undo retains up to 20 changes during the session. Board capacity starts at eight sheets; the 40-credit **Larger personal pinboard** expands it to 24.

## Twenty-four incidental objects

Six artifacts are placed in distinct filing drawers in each wing, deterministically from the save seed. Cabinets have no exterior collectible indication. Open a drawer and scroll past its originals, moved-file notices and printed copies. An object, if present, is beneath the last file. An emptied drawer can still contain its object.

Objects go directly into the collection compartment and catalogue without using document inventory space. Identification costs four credits and reveals a name and its ink illustration; it is optional. Desk correspondence offers free contextual leads and an explicit exact-drawer request. No case completion is required to collect anything.

Milestones at 4/12/20 objects unlock three cosmetic stamps; 8/16 unlock desk accessories; 24 unlocks a certificate. Select a stamp in Collection and apply it explicitly from a reader. It does not search a file, uncover evidence, earn money or complete the assignment. File drawers remain available for actual files.

## Internal mail

Use the vintage workstation at the W4 desk. Its bulky CRT and keyboard face the chair; the tower is beneath the desk. You can activate it from either side. The computer opens a period desktop and mail window with folder buttons and working Mailbox, Correspondence and Enclosures commands. Inbox, Sent, Drafts and Compose are local to the game. Select Tess, Martin, Facilities or Accounts, then a case and subject. Choose one of three authored responses on the sheet beside the monitor. Locked responses explain which physical records are needed. An optional personal note is retained verbatim; it is not interpreted. The selected response and attachments determine the reply. The right-hand reply control stays attached to the currently open case.

Six correspondence cases refer to existing stories: Cold war, The lunch market, The employee who never arrived, The tea budget, The photocopier queue and The perfect survey. Submit two different uncovered evidence records from the relevant story to resolve its correspondence case. Opening a workbook alone is insufficient: uncover its evidence through the existing cell/comment tools. Evidence found before this expansion counts. Original story rewards continue to follow the original rules; email case resolutions do not pay them twice.

Replies arrive after 30–90 seconds of active shift time. Reading, sketching and composing count; pause, onboarding, hidden tabs and focus loss do not. Pending replies survive reloads; no offline delivery occurs. New mail lights the terminal indicator and sounds quietly nearby. Read it at the computer. Unopened filing references in mail identify where to explore rather than revealing their contents remotely.

Martin’s existing evidence thresholds remain 1/3/6 records. Existing exchanges migrate as read mail with previously earned hints retained. New hints become available when his reply is opened. Repeating an unchanged enquiry reuses its thread.

## Office life and receipts

Public notices remain separate from the personal board. Twelve notices include four small fourth-wall jokes. The six-part blue-mug dispute develops with discoveries and resolved correspondence cases, without deadlines.

Shift log and Your desk offer a downloadable receipt. It includes existing shift totals and new collection/correspondence counts. Notes written and mail sent start when stationery is issued; earlier activity is not reconstructed. The completion screen also links to the receipt.

## Save and rendering contracts

The additive `deskLife.version: 3` state includes notes, sketch strokes, board references, collection, correspondence, cosmetics, response history and a Snake best score. Version-1 and version-2 desk data migrate automatically, preserving notes, sketches, pins, links, mail, drafts, rewards and scores. Their raw source is backed up once under `archive-office-before-stationery-v3` before writing the migration. Failed backup retains the existing paused-write/export recovery flow. Archive save/content/placement versions and the local save key remain unchanged. The raw pre-expansion save is backed up once as `archive-office-before-desk-life` before migration is written. Failed backup pauses automatic writes and offers export; writing remains in memory until a backup can be downloaded.

Notes are stored once, referenced by board pins. Limits are 160 strokes and 10,000 points per sheet, 60,000 points across the notebook, 96 strings, one draft and a bounded correspondence binder. Text is escaped before insertion. The board and public notices use cached canvas textures updated on changes. Fixed terminal and shelf solids are batched with vertex colours; found objects are instanced, and the screen adds no live light. Drawing surfaces reuse the original whiteboard stroke painter while retaining independent save data.

## Desktop expansion

Files contains Shared, Accounts, Facilities, Personal and Old Backups. Its 24 local documents include two leads or alternate drafts for each of the six correspondence cases and twelve office-life documents. Alternate case drafts unlock after opening a related physical original, or resolving the case. Digital extracts can be clipped to a notebook sheet; they carry a DIGITAL source label, never grant evidence, and never trigger assignment completion.

The Directory links the four contacts to their cases. Print Queue reads the existing printer's input/output and active-job state, and links to the physical controls. It does not print arbitrary digital files.

Snake is optional. Focus its board and use arrows/WASD, Space to pause, or the visible buttons. Leaving the app disposes its timer; losing focus pauses it until resumed. Only the personal best survives reopening. Coworker scores are fictional and no credits or investigation rewards are awarded.

A case can move from enquiry to discussion when you open a related original or read a reply, and to resolution when the existing evidence requirements are satisfied. Clarification, evidence and humorous replies have different authored text. They never permanently close a case. Unresolved cases may send one gentle follow-up three active minutes after a previously read reply became due. Paused, hidden and offline time do not advance the shift.

Desk data now uses version 2, adding response history, digital excerpts and a Snake best score. Version-1 data migrates without reconstructing or rewriting earlier correspondence. The raw save is backed up under `archive-office-before-desktop-v2` before automatic writes; backup failure leaves writes paused and export available. An old draft retains its note and enclosures and asks for an authored response before sending.


## Sticky notes and equipment

The pinboard contains a Sticky pad stack and a Notebook sheets stack. Use **+** beside Sticky pad, write in the inline paper editor, and choose Done. The note is placed in a free board position automatically. Undo restores the text present when the editor opened. An unfinished draft is retained. No pad, thread spool or marker needs equipping; the former stationery-box model remains in the source but is no longer placed in the office.

Sticky capacity remains 8, or 24 with the existing capacity upgrade. The colour upgrade still unlocks rose, blue and sage. Select a board note to edit it. Drag or use arrow keys to move it. Its corner cross returns it to the saved stack, preserving its writing; choosing that stack entry pins it again. The Notebook sheets **+** opens saved, unpinned sheets for placement.

Thread enables click-to-connect: select two notes, optionally choose a colour and label, and use the small cross beside a saved connection to remove it. Escape cancels an unfinished connection or closes the inline editor before leaving the board. The 96-connection limit and typed note IDs remain unchanged.

Whiteboards are writable immediately and always use the current room's saved drawing. There is no room picker or marker-equipment gate. Existing drawings, paper notes, loose stickies and legacy held objects retain the same save format.

## Office time and refreshments

Office time starts at **08:00 on Monday, 8 January 2007**, then adds saved active shift duration. Wall clocks, HUD and the W4 flip calendar agree. Reading, notebook editing and computer apps count; explicit breaks, onboarding, hidden tabs, focus loss and offline time do not. Calendar dates roll at office midnight and never follow the device date.

A cream-and-green drinks machine stands against W2's back wall, with a can-recycling bin alongside. Archive Cola, Orange Overtime and Still Employed sparkling water are free. Select a drink, wait for its short active-time dispense cycle, then collect it from the tray. Click or press F while holding the can to drink; the empty can stays in your hand. Inventory can stow it, and the machine or bin recycles it. There is one outstanding can at a time, including pending output or a stored can; reload retains that state. Drinks grant no credits, evidence, energy or investigation advantage.
